# Missing Person — Strengthening My Digital Investigation & OSINT Skills

**Platform:** TryHackMe  
**Room:** Missing Person  
**Category:** OSINT, Reverse Image Search, Digital Investigation  
**Date:** 28 July 2026

After completing OhSINT and Cache Me Outside, I wanted to continue improving my Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) skills through a more realistic investigation. Missing Person felt like the perfect next step because it combined reverse image searching, metadata analysis, geolocation, social media investigation, and digital footprint analysis into a single investigation.

I started by examining the evidence provided instead of making assumptions. The challenge included a small collection of images and a short message about a missing traveler. Rather than searching randomly, I carefully analyzed each image to identify useful clues that could help narrow down the investigation.

While exploring the images, I used reverse image search to identify the racing circuit shown in one of the photographs. This provided the first major breakthrough by revealing both the location and the event associated with the image. From there, I continued researching publicly available information, building a timeline of the victim's journey, and identifying places that appeared throughout the investigation.

As I progressed through the room, I inspected image metadata, searched for restaurants and event locations, investigated local businesses, and followed information across multiple public sources. Every new clue helped answer the next question, and I became more comfortable correlating information instead of relying on a single website or search result.

Towards the end of the investigation, I researched the MotoGP after-party, identified the local DJ, located the cave mentioned in the final message, and eventually discovered publicly available contact information through social media. The room reinforced how small pieces of information from different sources can be combined to reconstruct someone's digital footprint.

Compared to the previous OSINT rooms, Missing Person focused much more on reverse image searching, metadata analysis, geolocation, and building a logical timeline. Every stage depended on careful observation and connecting multiple pieces of public information together, making it one of the most realistic OSINT investigations I have completed so far.

## Tools I Used

- Google Search
- Google Images (Reverse Image Search)
- Image Metadata Viewer
- Google Maps
- Social Media Platforms
- Browser

## What I Learned

- Reverse image searching is an effective technique for identifying unknown locations.
- Image metadata can provide valuable information such as timestamps.
- Small visual details often become important investigative clues.
- Public social media profiles can reveal additional information during investigations.
- OSINT investigations rely on verifying information rather than making assumptions.
- Building a timeline helps organize findings and connect related evidence.
- Successful investigations require patience, observation, and logical thinking.

## Final Thoughts

Missing Person strengthened many of the OSINT concepts I learned in previous challenges while introducing more practical techniques such as reverse image searching, metadata analysis, geolocation, and timeline reconstruction. More than anything, it reinforced that successful investigations are driven by careful observation, validating information from multiple public sources, and patiently connecting small pieces of evidence to uncover the complete picture.

Looking forward to building on these skills with more OSINT challenges.
